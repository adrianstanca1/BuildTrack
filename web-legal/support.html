Frequently Asked Questions

What is BuildTrack?

BuildTrack is a construction project management app for iOS and Android. Track projects, tasks, safety incidents, inspections, and team members — all in one place.

Is it free?

Yes. BuildTrack is completely free with full access to all features.

Does it work offline?

Yes. BuildTrack syncs with the cloud but caches data locally so you can continue working without an internet connection.

How do I contact support?

Email support@cortexbuildpro.com and we'll respond within 24 hours.
