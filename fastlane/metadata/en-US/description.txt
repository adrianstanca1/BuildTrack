BuildTrack is the all-in-one construction project management app for teams on the go. Plan projects, assign tasks, report safety incidents, conduct inspections, and manage your workforce — all from your phone or tablet.

KEY FEATURES:
• Project Tracking — Create and monitor construction projects with geolocation
• Task Management — Assign, schedule, and track task completion
• Safety Reports — Log incidents with photos, severity ratings, and follow-up actions
• Inspections — Record site inspections with checklists and photo evidence
• Team Management — Add workers, assign roles, and track participation
• Interactive Map — View all your project sites on a live map
• Real-time Sync — Data syncs across all devices instantly
• Offline Mode — Continue working without internet; sync when back online
• Dark Mode — Easy on the eyes for late-night site visits
• Push Notifications — Stay informed about task updates and safety alerts

WHY BUILDTRACK?
Built for construction professionals who need reliable tools that work on-site, offline, and across teams. No spreadsheets, no paper trails — everything in one app.
